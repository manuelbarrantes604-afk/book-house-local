Where you'll be

Staying in Bentonville, Arkansas

Close to It All sits in Bentonville, the Northwest Arkansas town known for art, trails and the Walmart Home Office. Most of what visitors come for is a short drive away; guests describe everything as less than ten minutes by car.

Nearby

  • Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art: about 2 miles away. General admission to the permanent collection is free.
  • Bentonville Square: about 1.3 miles away, with restaurants, coffee and the Walmart Museum.
  • Walmart Home Office: about 1.2 miles away, handy for work trips and vendor visits.
  • Razorback Regional Greenway: under a mile from the house: a paved trail linking Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale and Fayetteville for biking and walking.
  • Mountain bike trails: Bentonville’s trail network, including Slaughter Pen, is a short drive away.

Need a home in Springdale instead? See Villa Nica in Springdale.

Staying in Springdale, Arkansas

Villa Nica is near downtown Springdale, in the middle of Northwest Arkansas, so Bentonville, Rogers and Fayetteville are all within reach by car. It is across from Springdale High School and a minute from the library.

Nearby

  • Downtown Springdale and Shiloh Square: about a 15-minute walk to Walter Turnbow Park, Emma Avenue’s restaurants and the Shiloh Museum of Ozark History.
  • Razorback Regional Greenway: about half a mile away: the paved trail runs through downtown Springdale toward Fayetteville and Bentonville.
  • Arvest Ballpark: home of the Northwest Arkansas Naturals, a short drive away (about 3.6 miles).
  • Bentonville and Crystal Bridges: about 13 miles north, for a day of art and trails.
  • University of Arkansas, Fayetteville: about 8.5 miles south, handy for Razorback game weekends.
